# Vision — Hey Hero

Hey Hero is an **AI Life Operating System**, not just a reminder or chatbot app.

It combines your personal data with live information from the web so conversations feel useful every day.

## Product promise

Say **"Hey Hero"** and get help with:

- Today's schedule, habits, and weather
- AI-generated workouts based on sleep & recovery
- Work context (email, Jira, GitHub)
- Personalized news (AI, cloud, DevOps, sports, finance…)
- Long-term memory of preferences and routines

## Conversation examples

### Greeting
- **You:** Hey Hero!
- **Hero:** Hey Vishnu! 👋 What can I help you with today?

### Daily update
- **You:** Hey Hero, what's today's update?
- **Hero:** Meetings, water reminder, workout, emails, Jira, cloud alerts, weather…

### Workout
- **You:** Hey Hero, generate today's workout.
- **Hero:** Plan based on previous workouts, sleep, and recovery.

### Progress
- **You:** Hey Hero, how am I doing this week?
- **Hero:** Workouts, steps, sleep, water consistency summary.

### Tech news
- **You:** Hey Hero, what's new in AI today?
- **Hero:** Top stories filtered to your interests.

### Morning briefing
Good morning with meetings, workout, sleep, cloud/AI news, Jira, email, and weather in one message.

## Why the name works

**Hey Hero** sounds like a real personal assistant and is broad enough to grow beyond fitness or reminders into a full productivity + wellness product.
